01

Strap on the board

The ESP32 and its accelerometer read the arm as you lift. Pick the exercise on the board or on the site — they stay in sync over Bluetooth.

02

Lift and get scored

A rep counts only when you reach the top of the movement. Each rep is graded on tempo: good, too fast, or too slow.

03

Save it to your name

Sign in and every set saves to your account — reps, the good / fast / slow breakdown, and rep-by-rep history. It follows you across devices.

Everything here is built and working: six arm exercises scored by the board, and accounts that save your sessions. Open a row for the detail.

Workouts Live

Six arm exercises, one page. The ESP32's accelerometer counts a rep when you reach the top of the movement and scores each rep on tempo — good, too fast, or too slow. Pick a movement below or on the workout page; the board follows over Bluetooth either way. All six exercises are calibrated and calculated out of the box; you can also re-calibrate on the board anytime.

  • Bicep curl Live
  • Overhead extension Live
  • Lateral raise Live
  • Tricep pushdown Live
  • Pec deck Live
  • Shoulder press Live
Start a workout
Accounts Live

Create an account and sign in. Every session saves to your name: the exercise, rep count, the good / too fast / too slow breakdown, and rep-by-rep history. It saves when you press Save session, switch exercises, or leave the page. On the deployed site your data is stored server-side and follows you across devices.

Open account
Live preview Live

No board handy? The workout page has a Preview button that plays sample reps for the selected exercise so you can see the scoring, real-time acceleration, and the arm animation without hardware.

Open a row to see the stat. Live ones are on the site now. The rest are ready for the next sensor, and the last row is an empty slot.

Today Live

The home board. Rings for the day, then heart rate, steps, active minutes, calories, and the current workout.

Open the board
Heart rate Planned

Beats per minute on the watch face and on the day board. The number on the home page is a preview until a pulse sensor is on the board.

Steps Planned

Steps since midnight, from a wrist or ankle accelerometer. The card is on the board and waiting for that count.

Active minutes Planned

Minutes of movement, plus the calorie estimate that sits next to them. Both cards are on Today.

Workouts Live

A workout is its own session. Curl form is the first one: the arm sensor counts reps and scores the tempo. The next workout gets its own row and its own page.

Start curl form
Distance Planned

How far the body or the weight travels, from an ultrasonic sensor. The card is already on Today. It stays blank until that code is flashed.

Sleep Planned

Hours down and how still the wrist was. Not on the board yet. Add the card when the sensor exists.

New stat Open slot

Empty on purpose. Copy this block in features.html, name the stat, and mark it Live or Planned. If it has a page, add a link. Then add a card for it on the home board.
